## SLIDE 6: Innovation 1 — SensorWatch (Passive Telemetry Engine)

**Slide Title:** SensorWatch: Autonomous Road Hazard Telemetry
**Subtitle:** Converting Vehicle Shocks into Cryptographic Civic Evidence
**Visual Layout:** Split Technical Layout (Oscilloscope Graphic on Left, Physics & Crypto on Right)
**Visual Asset:** `pptimgs/06_sensorwatch_telemetry.png` (50Hz acceleration spike graph, motorcycle wheel on cracked road, and SHA-256 cryptographic padlock seal)

### On-Slide Content:
* **Hardware-Fused Sensing:**
  - Samples `userAccelerometerEventStream` at 50Hz with gravity mathematically removed by hardware fusion.
* **Dynamic Shock Detection Threshold:**
  - Calculates instantaneous 3D Euclidean magnitude: $a_{\text{net}} = \sqrt{a_x^2 + a_y^2 + a_z^2}$.
  - Triggers only when linear acceleration spike exceeds **$2.5g$**.
* **Smart Noise & False-Positive Filters:**
  - **GPS Speed Deadband:** Requires vehicle speed $\ge 3\text{ km/h}$ to ignore foot tapping, phone drops, or walking.
  - **Adaptive Cooldown:** 1,500ms debounce cooldown prevents multi-triggering over a single road joint.
  - **Rolling Baseline Filter:** Continuously discounts normal road vibration textures.
* **Cryptographic Evidence Package:**
  - Automatically captures GPS coordinates, speed, timestamp, and peak shock waveform.
  - Computes a deterministic **SHA-256 cryptographic hash** stored in Supabase, preventing manual tampering or spoofing.

> **Key Formula:** $a_{\text{net}} \ge 2.5g \quad \land \quad v_{\text{vehicle}} \ge 3\text{ km/h} \implies \text{SHA-256 Telemetry Package Sealed}$

---

### Speaker Script (Slide 6):
*"Our first breakthrough innovation is **SensorWatch**.*

*Imagine commuting to work on your two-wheeler. Your phone is resting in a handlebar mount. With SensorWatch activated, the phone's accelerometer runs at 50 samples per second.*

*When your vehicle hits a sunken manhole or severe pothole, the phone experiences a violent vertical acceleration. Our algorithm computes the 3-axis vector magnitude. If that spike crosses 2.5 times the force of gravity, and the GPS confirms you are traveling at vehicle speed above 3 kilometers per hour, it flags a road defect.*

*To eliminate false alarms, we implemented an adaptive rolling baseline and a 1,500-millisecond cooldown so one pothole doesn't trigger five separate reports.*

*Most importantly, the moment an impact occurs, Nivara seals an immutable **SHA-256 evidence package**. This includes the raw sensor waveform, exact GPS coordinates, vehicle speed, and a timestamp. When city authorities view this ticket, they are not looking at a vague complaint—they are looking at irrefutable, cryptographically sealed physics."*
